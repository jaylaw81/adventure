# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Creators: aspiring storytellers, writers, educators, and game designers who want to build branching choose-your-own-adventure stories. They sit down with a narrative in mind and need a visual canvas to map scenes, choices, and outcomes. They range from first-timers experimenting with interactive fiction to prolific authors managing multi-chapter works. Secondary audience: readers who discover and play published stories.

Primary design focus is the creator: the editor, dashboard, and story management surfaces.

## Product Purpose

StoryQuestor is a web-based platform for creating, publishing, and playing branching interactive stories. Creators use a visual node-canvas to build non-linear narratives by placing scene nodes and drawing choice connections. AI-generated scene illustrations, chapter organization, and a public reader make the full loop from idea to published story possible in one place.

Success looks like: a creator opens the editor, builds a story, and feels the tool got out of their way and let the story happen.

## Brand Personality

Creative, energetic, approachable. The tool should feel alive and capable without being intimidating. It earns trust by being visually polished and functionally smooth, not by looking serious.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS: Notion-clone aesthetics, white card grids, blue primary buttons, neutral gray everything. StoryQuestor is a creative tool for storytellers, not a productivity dashboard for office workers.

## Design Principles

1. **The canvas is the stage** — the creator's story takes center; UI chrome recedes, narrative content leads.
2. **Energy without overwhelm** — vibrant and alive, but never chaotic; complexity reveals itself progressively, not all at once.
3. **Approachable depth** — a first-timer can publish their first scene in minutes; a power user discovers more over time. Both are served.
4. **Craft signals trust** — a polished, intentional interface tells creators this tool is worthy of their best ideas.
5. **Delight in the process** — building a story should feel like play. Small moments of satisfaction matter.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline. Audience controls (All Ages / Teens / Adults Only) are a product feature. No additional stated accommodation requirements beyond standard contrast, focus management, and keyboard navigability.
